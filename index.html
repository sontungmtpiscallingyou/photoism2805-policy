📄 Privacy Policy

App: Photoism: Idol Photobooth
Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Welcome to Photoism: Idol Photobooth ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our services.

1. Camera & Media Permissions

Local Processing Only: Your camera is used solely in real time to capture photobooth pictures and videos with your selected idol frames.

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to show the live camera preview, capture photobooth photos, and record short reaction videos side-by-side with idol frames.
  • No Remote Upload: All photo captures, video recordings, stickers, and frame edits are processed 100% locally on your device. We do NOT transmit, upload, or store your photos, videos, or facial images on external servers.
  • Device Storage: When you export or save your finished photostrip or video, it is saved directly to your device's local gallery / storage.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our app does not require user registration or account creation. We do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
  • User account passwords or login credentials.
  • Biometric data, facial recognition profiles, or sensitive personal data.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support free features, the application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect anonymous device information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect anonymous advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID) and non-personally identifiable diagnostic information to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more details.
  • Google Play In-App Updates: Checks for the latest version of the app on Google Play to ensure you always have access to new features and security improvements.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for general audiences and idol fan communities.

5. Security

Because all media creation and photo editing happen entirely on your local device without cloud transmission, your personal creations remain in your own possession and control at all times.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document along with an updated "Last Updated" date.
